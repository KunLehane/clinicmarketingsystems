# Clinic case study sources

## Physio Rooms Cork

The project story comes directly from Aidan: meeting James in Douglas beside one of his other businesses, James returning a few months later for a new website, the completed launch and preparations for the next stage of marketing. No new booking, revenue, ranking or traffic results are claimed.

Live sources inspected on 4 November 2026:

- https://physioroomscork.com/ : current live site. The www address redirects here. Names James Malone as founder, shows Douglas Court, Ballinlough and Carrigaline, treatment navigation, team profiles, patient reviews, insurance information, booking and WhatsApp links. Footer credits PayPerClick.ie. Desktop design uses dark sections, prominent headings and muted green buttons.
- https://physioroomscork.com/treatments/shockwave-therapy/ : treatment explanation, FAQs, reviews, booking links and an enquiry card asking where it hurts.
- https://physioroomscork.com/corporate-physiotherapy/ : workplace ergonomics, on-site physiotherapy and injury prevention, with employer enquiry options.
- https://physio-rooms-cork.uk1.cliniko.com/bookings : the website's booking destination. Opens a location selector including the three public Cork clinics. No appointment was booked and no enquiry was submitted.

The initially supplied candidate physioroomscork.ie did not resolve in the browser (DNS_PROBE_FINISHED_NXDOMAIN). Search results pointed to the .com domain, which was then inspected directly. Search snippets still described an older site, so the new case study uses the live browser content rather than those snippets.

## Existing studies and identity checks

Both /case-studies/magic-hands-jimmy/ and /case-studies/neuro-pain-clinic/ existed before this change. Their historical metrics and delivery claims were retained, not newly inferred or independently revalidated from client homepages.

- https://magichands.ie/ and https://magichands.ie/about-us/ identify Magic Hands Jimmy, the Disha family, Jimmy and his two sons, and a manual therapy practice in Clonsilla, Dublin 15. The existing case study incorrectly labelled the practice Cork; the label, listing and metadata are corrected. A live-site link is added.
- https://neuropainclinic.ie/about-us/ has the title "About Us - Neuro Muscular Pain And Injury Clinic" and explicitly calls the business "The Neuro Pain Clinic", founded by Will Donohoe, in Dublin 15. https://neuropainclinic.ie/ identifies its Castleknock location and neuromuscular services. This is direct evidence that these two public names refer to the same practice. The existing case study is retained with a short identity clarification and live link rather than creating a duplicate.

The separately recorded generic name "Neuromuscular Clinic" does not by itself identify a client. No recent upgrade, payment or photo-work claims from that private note have been attached to Neuro Pain Clinic. If a different business was intended by that shorthand, its exact name or URL is needed before adding another case study.
