Clinic marketing, run by someone who measures it

Clinic Marketing Systems is run by Aidan Lehane of PayPerClick.ie, with over 10 years of Google Ads and local SEO work for Irish businesses. It exists because clinics deserve marketing that is judged on booked appointments.

A focused offer for a specific kind of business

We work with physiotherapy, chiropractic, dental and addiction treatment clinics in Ireland. Clinics share a set of problems that general marketing agencies tend to miss: bookings happen in separate software, advertising rules are stricter, and patients need clear, careful information before they commit.

Clinic Marketing Systems brings the website, local search, Google Ads and booking tracking together into one system for that kind of business, and reports on it in the terms a clinic owner actually cares about.
