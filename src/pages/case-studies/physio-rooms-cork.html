Physio Rooms Cork: a new website for the next stage

A conversation in Douglas led to a new website for James and the team at Physio Rooms Cork. The site is now live, bringing their treatments, team and three Cork clinics together with clear routes to book.

The starting point

We first met James, the owner of Physio Rooms Cork, in Douglas, beside one of his other businesses. A few months later, he came back to us looking for a new website for the clinic. We built it, and we are really happy with how it turned out.

What we built

  • Dedicated treatment pages for services including physiotherapy, shockwave therapy, sports massage and dry needling
  • Body-area pages and condition guides, so patients can start with what hurts rather than having to know the name of a treatment
  • Location pages for Douglas Court, Ballinlough and Carrigaline, with addresses and directions
  • Prominent booking buttons linking to Cliniko, alongside WhatsApp, phone and enquiry options for people who have a question first
  • Team profiles, patient reviews and insurance information alongside the practical details patients need
  • A workplace physiotherapy section covering ergonomics, on-site care and injury prevention for Cork employers

A clear next step for patients

The design pairs dark sections and clear headings with muted green booking buttons. Patients can explore a treatment, find their nearest clinic or go straight to online booking. Those who are not ready to book can ask a question through WhatsApp or an enquiry form.

For a clinic owner, that means the website can explain the care you offer without making the next step hard to find.

What comes next

The new website is live. We are now getting ready to take the clinic's marketing to the next level and help James push the business forward. This is the website launch story, with the next stage of marketing still ahead.
