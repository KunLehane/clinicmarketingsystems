Neuro Pain Clinic: from a Wix site to a full booking system

A physiotherapy and neuromuscular therapy clinic that was losing its own name on Google. We rebuilt the website, the local search presence and the Google Ads, then tracked every booking action.

The starting point

Neuro Pain Clinic also uses the name Neuro Muscular Pain and Injury Clinic on its website. Its About page identifies the practice as Will Donohoe's clinic in Dublin 15.

The clinic ran on a Wix website and was being outranked for its own brand name, both in the normal results and on Google Maps. Patients searching for the clinic by name could land on someone else.

What we built

  • A new website planned around the searches patients actually make, with current contact details and a proper booking flow
  • A local SEO plan that won back the brand name on Google and Maps first, then physio and neuromuscular therapy terms across the surrounding areas
  • Blog content written from real search data, answering what people were typing in
  • Conversion tracking through Google Tag Manager, so every call, booking click and enquiry is counted
  • Tightly focused Google Ads campaigns, switched on only once the tracking was proven

Why it worked

Ads were the last step, not the first. Once the website ranked for the clinic's own name and the tracking was counting real booking actions, every euro of ad spend could be judged on bookings rather than clicks.

The result is a clinic with one connected system: a site that ranks, a Google Business Profile that wins its area, and reports that show which channel produced which booking. That is the same system we now set up for every clinic we work with.
