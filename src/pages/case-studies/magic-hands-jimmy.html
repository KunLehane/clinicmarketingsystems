Magic Hands Jimmy: turning 100 million TikTok views into bookings

A manual therapy practice with a huge social following and nowhere for that attention to book. We built the website and Google presence that turned brand searches into appointments.

Get the same check for your clinic Book a call

The starting point

Jimmy had built over 100 million views on TikTok, but there was no proper place to send people who wanted to book. Hundreds of Google searches for the brand went unanswered every day, landing on social profiles instead of a page the practice owned.

What we built

  • A professional website designed around one job: getting a visitor into a booking
  • Booking platform integration, so traffic flows straight through to an appointment
  • Google Maps optimisation, so brand searches land on the practice, not a social profile
  • Local SEO foundations to build on when the practice wants to grow beyond brand demand

Why it worked

The demand already existed. The practice was losing it at the last step. Jimmy and his two sons, who run the business together, now own the platform their audience lands on, instead of renting it from a social app.

The site works around the clock on brand searches alone, with no ad spend. Google Ads and wider SEO are held in reserve for when the practice chooses to grow beyond its existing audience.
