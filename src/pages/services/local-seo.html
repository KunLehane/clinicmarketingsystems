What is included

The groundwork, done properly and kept up

Local search rewards clinics whose information is complete, consistent and active. Most of the work is not complicated, but it has to be done carefully and kept up to date.

  • Google Business Profile audit and setup for each location
  • Correct primary and secondary categories, services and hours
  • Booking links that point to the right place and can be tracked
  • Photo guidance and regular profile updates
  • A simple, compliant process for asking patients for reviews
  • Help writing replies to reviews, positive and negative
  • Consistent name, address and phone details across directories
  • Location and service pages on your site that support each profile

Multi-location clinics

One profile and one page per location

Each clinic location needs its own verified Business Profile and its own page on your website, with its own details and booking link. We set this up so each location can be found and tracked on its own.

Questions about local SEO

Can you guarantee a top position in Maps?

No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Google decides rankings. What we can do is make sure your profile and website give it every reason to show your clinic.

Do you buy or write reviews?

Never. Fake or incentivised reviews break Google's rules and can get a profile suspended. We help you ask real patients in a simple, compliant way.
