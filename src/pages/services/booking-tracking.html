Where clinic tracking usually breaks

A patient clicks an ad, lands on your website, then taps "Book now" and moves to your booking software. If that step is not set up carefully, the booking is recorded with no source, or not recorded at all. Your reports then show traffic, but not which traffic books.

Clinic booking tools such as Cliniko and Jane support Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics, but each has its own setup and its own quirks, especially when the booking page is embedded in your site.

Questions about booking tracking

Do you see patient information?

No patient health information is needed. Tracking records that a booking happened and where the visitor came from. We set it up so no names, contact details or clinical details are sent to Google.

What about patients who phone to book?

Phone bookings can be tracked with call tracking and, where it makes sense, by recording how the patient heard about you in your booking software.

Is this worth doing if I do not run ads?

Yes. Knowing whether bookings come from Maps, search, social or referrals tells you where to put your time, even with no ad budget.
