Open guides compared with a leaflet library

Patient leaflet libraries are often sold to clinics as lead magnets: a patient enters an email address and gets a PDF. That trade suits the email list more than the patient, and it hides your clinic's knowledge from the searches that bring new patients in.

Written by us, approved by your clinicians

We handle research, writing, formatting and publishing. Your team checks every guide for clinical accuracy before it goes live, and can ask for changes at any point.

Guides explain conditions and what to expect from an appointment in plain language. They do not promise outcomes or make claims about treatments, and they always encourage patients to get properly assessed.

  1. Plan the libraryWe agree a list of guides based on the conditions you see most and what patients search for.
  2. DraftWe research and write each guide in plain, factual language.
  3. Clinical reviewYour clinician reviews and approves, or sends back changes.
  4. Publish and linkPublished on your site with structured data, internal links and a booking call to action.
  5. Keep currentGuides are reviewed and updated so the information stays accurate.
