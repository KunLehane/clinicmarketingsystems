Without booking data, Google optimises for the wrong thing

Google's automated bidding learns from the conversions you report. If a conversion is a page view or a button click, it finds more people who click. If a conversion is a completed booking, it finds more people who book.

Questions about Google Ads

Who owns the Google Ads account?

You do. We work inside your account, so the data and history stay with your clinic.

Are there restrictions on advertising healthcare?

Yes. Google has policies on healthcare and medicines, and some treatments and services, including addiction treatment, can have extra requirements. We work within them and tell you up front if something cannot be advertised.

How much should a clinic spend?

It depends on your area, your treatments and how many new patients you can take. We talk this through on a call before suggesting a number.
