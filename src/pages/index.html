The problem

Most clinic marketing reports stop at the click

Clinic owners are shown traffic, impressions and cost per click. What they actually want to know is simpler: how many people booked, and what made them book.

Clicks are not patients

An ad can get plenty of clicks and fill no appointments. Without booking data, nobody can tell a campaign that works from one that only spends.

Booking happens elsewhere

Most clinics take bookings in Cliniko, Jane or similar software on a separate page. That handoff is where tracking usually breaks and the source of the booking is lost.

Nobody owns the whole journey

One person built the website, another runs the ads, and the Business Profile was set up years ago. Each part looks fine on its own while patients drop out between them.

Patient guide library

Patient guides belong on your website, not behind an email gate

Some clinic marketing providers offer a library of patient leaflets that visitors can only read after handing over an email address. It looks tidy, but a gated PDF is invisible to Google and an extra hurdle for a patient who just wants an answer.

We write condition and treatment guides as open pages on your own site. Your clinicians review every guide before it goes live. Patients find you when they search, read something useful, and book with a clinic they already trust.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need to change my booking software?

No. We work with the booking software you already use, such as Cliniko or Jane. Part of the setup is connecting it to your website and your tracking so bookings can be counted properly.

Can I start with just one part, like Google Ads?

Yes. Many clinics start with the part that is hurting most. We will usually check your tracking first, because ads and SEO are hard to judge without it.

Who writes the patient guides, and who signs them off?

We research and write them. Your clinicians review and approve every guide before it is published, and we keep the copy factual, with no claims about treatment outcomes.

Do you work with clinics outside Ireland?

We are focused on clinics in Ireland first. If you run a clinic in the UK or US, get in touch and we can talk it through.
